About JAI Conclave 2026

JAI Conclave 2026 is the flagship youth diplomacy and public policy forum of VISHWAM, dedicated to fostering informed dialogue, leadership, and intellectual engagement among students, scholars, young professionals, policymakers, diplomats, academicians, and industry leaders.

The conclave provides a distinguished platform for discussions on governance, public policy, diplomacy, international affairs, economics, and leadership, encouraging the exchange of ideas and collaboration between emerging and established leaders.

How To Participate

1. Thematic Councils (Deliberation Track)

Designed on structured deliberative formats similar to high-level academic simulations. Participants will engage in moderated discussions, debates, negotiations, and policy-driven dialogue across thematic councils.

Best suited for: Public policy, international relations, governance, law & debate
2. Art Orbit (Creative Track)

A curated space for artistic expression across mediums. Participants may exhibit artwork or opt for a live workshop for their creative skills. Formats: Painting, sketching, digital art, photography, calligraphy, sculpture, performance art, visual forms.

3. MINT (Entrepreneurship Track)

A platform for emerging entrepreneurs. Selected participants receive visibility, interaction opportunities, and a platform to present, promote, and offer products or services.

Ideal for: Student founders, innovators, creators & early-stage ventures
✨ Ancillary Activities (Open to All Participants)
1. SHABRI: A Civilizational Cultural Evening reflecting devotion, inclusion, and cultural continuity.
2. FoE Mic: Open Mic for Emerging Youth Voices — spoken word, poetry, and fearless expression.
